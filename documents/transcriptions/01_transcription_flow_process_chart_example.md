The wood is partly purchased from a supplier and partly purchased in the company's own forest. The wood is conveyed through an artificial channel to the covered storage area. From there, it is taken to be cut in the chipper, where the dimensions are made uniform to the standard size of one-fourth of an inch. Wood chips that are too large are returned to the previous station and those that are too small are removed. The chips are then stored in silos. Lignin is removed from wood chips by boiling them in caustic soda and sodium sulfide solution. Then the chips pass into a low-pressure discharge vat to separate the fibers. The wood fibers are then centrifuged and pressed. The pulp produced in this process is finally subjected to bleaching and then put to rest in the warehouse. The paper is produced starting from the pulp in a dedicated machine in which the pulp is deprived of part of its content in water and compressed in steam-heated cylinders. Then it is wrapped in largely reels and stored. When delivered to the customer, it is cut and rewound into smaller reels. This is the text.
